HOW IT WORKS

Claim a pixel. Support education.

Why this exists. Holy Pixel Wall is a small project to help raise money for university fees and education costs. Every pixel you buy supports that goal.

1. Drag to select free pixels.

2. Add your name and a photo, GIF, or video (tap to upload). Optional link & description.

3. Pay $0.50 / pixel for 6 months, or $1 / pixel for 1 year.

4. Your pixels stay locked — others cannot take them until time ends.

Hover any claim to see the owner name and description.

Photo tip: Use a clear JPG, PNG, GIF, or MP4/WebM. Photos stay high quality; GIFs and videos play on your block.

THE STORY

Why this wall exists

I want to go to university, but I don’t have the money for the fees.

Tuition, books, living costs — it adds up fast. Waiting for a miracle wasn’t working, so I built something small instead: this pixel wall.

Every pixel you claim helps cover education costs. You’re not just buying space on a grid — you’re helping someone stay in school.

Thank you for being here. It means more than you know.
